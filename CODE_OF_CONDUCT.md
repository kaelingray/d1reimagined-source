# Code of conduct

D1 Reimagined is a community preservation project. Everyone who takes part, on GitHub, Discord or elsewhere, is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people asking questions.
- Critique work, not people. No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination or sexual content.
- Keep discussion on topic and in good faith. No spam, self-promotion or deliberate disruption.
- Respect the law and other people's work. Never share or request game files, extracted assets, firmware, keys, account data or cheats.
- Respect privacy. Don't post other people's personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict or ban participants who break these rules.

Report problems to the moderators on the [community Discord](https://d1reimagined.com/discord) or privately to security@d1reimagined.com. Reports are handled confidentially.
